# DairoDFS 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 7 日

DairoDFS（以下简称“本 App”）是一款开源的文件管理客户端，用来连接你自己部署的 DairoDFS 服务器。本政策说明本 App 会处理哪些信息，以及这些信息去了哪里。

## 我们不收集你的任何信息

本 App 的开发者不运营任何收集用户数据的服务器。本 App：

- 不收集、不上传、不出售任何个人信息给开发者或第三方；
- 不包含广告，不包含任何第三方统计、分析或追踪 SDK；
- 不追踪你在其他 App 或网站上的活动。

## 你的数据只发送到你自己指定的服务器

使用本 App 时，你需要填写服务器地址、用户名和密码。本 App 只会和你填写的这台服务器通信，传输的内容包括：

- 登录时的用户名和密码，用来换取登录票据；
- 你浏览、上传、下载、移动、删除的文件和文件夹；
- 你主动选择上传的照片和视频；
- 一个由设备标识计算出的设备 ID，供服务器区分登录设备。

这台服务器由你自己或你的服务器管理员部署和管理，数据如何存储和保护，由服务器的管理者负责。本 App 的开发者无法访问这些数据。

## 保存在你设备上的信息

为了让 App 正常使用，以下信息只保存在你的设备上：

- 服务器地址、用户名和登录票据（不保存密码）；
- 主题、功能模式、下载数量等设置；
- 已浏览过的图片、视频、音乐等文件的缓存，以及你主动下载的文件。

你可以在 App 的“下载管理”中清理缓存。删除本 App 会同时删除以上所有信息。

## App 使用的权限

- **照片（读取）**：用来显示系统相册里的照片和视频，把你选择的内容上传到你的服务器，并在你确认后删除已上传的照片。
- **照片（添加）**：用来把服务器上的照片和视频保存到系统相册。
- **本地网络**：用来连接你部署在局域网内的服务器。

以上权限都只在你使用对应功能时才会请求，你可以随时在系统“设置”中关闭。

## 儿童隐私

本 App 不面向儿童收集任何信息。

## 政策更新

如果本政策有变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。

# DairoDFS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

DairoDFS ("the App") is an open-source file manager client that connects to a DairoDFS server you deploy yourself. This policy explains what information the App handles and where it goes.

## We do not collect your information

The developer of the App does not run any server that collects user data. The App:

- does not collect, upload, or sell any personal information to the developer or any third party;
- contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs;
- does not track your activity across other apps or websites.

## Your data is sent only to the server you specify

To use the App, you enter a server address, a username, and a password. The App communicates only with that server. The data sent includes:

- your username and password at sign-in, which are exchanged for a sign-in token;
- the files and folders you browse, upload, download, move, or delete;
- the photos and videos you choose to upload;
- a device ID derived from the device identifier, which the server uses to tell signed-in devices apart.

That server is deployed and managed by you or your server administrator, who is responsible for how the data is stored and protected. The developer of the App has no access to this data.

## Information stored on your device

For the App to work, the following information is stored only on your device:

- the server address, username, and sign-in token (your password is not stored);
- settings such as theme, mode, and download count;
- caches of images, videos, music, and other files you have viewed, and files you have downloaded.

You can clear the cache in Download Management in the App. Deleting the App deletes all of this information.

## Permissions

- **Photos (read)**: to show the photos and videos in your library, upload the items you choose to your server, and delete uploaded photos after you confirm.
- **Photos (add)**: to save photos and videos from your server to your photo library.
- **Local Network**: to connect to a server on your local network.

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature, and you can turn it off at any time in the system Settings.

## Children's privacy

The App does not collect any information from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
